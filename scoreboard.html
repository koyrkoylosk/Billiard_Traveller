What it does

Everything your club needs at the table

Players tap the screen, use their phone or a clicker. The rest happens on its own.

Scoring built for billiards

3-cushion carom and pool. Race to points, sets, 5-pins and the 4-player Survival format. Innings, high run, average, shot clock and extensions.

YouTube LIVE from the table

One tap and the match is on your channel, with a broadcast overlay: names, flags, score and run. If the connection drops, it reconnects by itself.

Camera replay & SUPER SHOT

Watch the last shot again on the screen. Save the best ones as SUPER SHOT clips, ready to share on social media.

Tournaments

Knockout draws, groups of up to 64 players and Mosconi-style team events. Matches are sent to the tables and results come back automatically.

Players & statistics

Player profiles with photo and flag, saved in the cloud. Every player can follow their own performance by month and year from a QR on their phone.

Your screen between games

When nobody plays, the screen shows your club logo, your sponsors, your SUPER SHOTs and fresh highlight videos from top billiard channels.

Table time & club tools

Table time tracking for billing, sales statistics and an admin PIN that keeps the settings in your hands.

ACADEMY training mode

Lessons on billiard systems and a practice mode with a voice coach in your headphones that counts made and missed shots for you.

Privacy built in

Players give their broadcast consent from their own phone with a QR code. Without it, they appear as “Player 1/2” and only the overhead camera is used.

Always up to date: new versions install themselves, signed and checked, when no match is being played. Remote support included.

Questions

Which games does it support?

3-cushion carom (points or sets), 5-pins, the 4-player Survival format, and pool.

Do I need internet?

Yes, for YouTube LIVE, cloud player profiles, tournaments and updates.

What about my players’ privacy?

Each player decides from their own phone whether their matches may be broadcast. They can withdraw at any time. Without consent, their name stays off the broadcast and only the overhead camera is used.

Which languages?

The scoreboard is available in English and Greek. More languages can be added for new markets.
